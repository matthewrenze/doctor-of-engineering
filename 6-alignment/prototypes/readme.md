# Certainty/uncertainty measures

## Token-level 
- Probability - higher is more certain
- Entropy - lower is more certain
- Margin - higher is more certain
## Sequence-level
- Min probability - higher is more certain
- Mean entropy - lower is more certain
- Max margin - higher is more certain
## Sample-based
- Self-consistency - higher is more certain
- Mean centroid similarity - higher is more certain
- Mean pairwise similarity - higher is more certain
- Robust pairwise similarity - higher is more certain
## Distribution-based
- Predictive entropy - lower is more certain
- Expected entropy - lower is more certain
- Mutual information - lower is more certain
