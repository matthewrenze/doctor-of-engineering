Here is a concise set of error categories distilled from the provided error reasons:

1. **Incorrect Cooking Method**  
   Errors involving using the wrong cooking action (e.g., frying instead of roasting, grilling skipped, or confusing cooking methods).

2. **Inventory Management Issues**  
   Errors due to poor handling of inventory limits, dropping/picking items repeatedly, inability to carry required items simultaneously, or losing track of dropped ingredients.

3. **Missing or Unavailable Ingredients**  
   Failures caused by inability to find, acquire, or substitute required ingredients, including searching explored areas without success.

4. **Recipe Step Execution Errors**  
   Not following recipe steps correctly, skipping steps, premature task termination, or arbitrary cooking without consulting the recipe.

5. **Object Interaction and Command Errors**  
   Incorrect or unrecognized commands, poor object referencing, inability to pick up or interact with items due to parsing or naming issues.

6. **Environment and Task Flow Handling**  
   Failures related to quitting/resetting the environment improperly, infinite loops in quitting or exploration, or not updating perception of environment state.

7. **Ingredient Handling and Preparation Loops**  
   Getting stuck in loops involving picking up/putting down items or tools, or mishandling cooking sequences causing repetitive ineffective actions.

---

These categories cover all error types and can be used to classify future agent errors succinctly.
