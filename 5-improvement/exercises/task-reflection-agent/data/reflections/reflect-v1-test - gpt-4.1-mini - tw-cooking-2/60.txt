When carrying multiple items and needing to pick up more, then plan inventory management ahead by dropping less immediately necessary items before picking up new ones to avoid repeated dropping and picking up.
When following a recipe with multiple ingredients and steps, then keep the cookbook accessible or take notes to avoid repeatedly re-examining it and losing time.
When the inventory limit is restrictive, then prioritize carrying essential tools (like the knife) and ingredients in the order they will be used to minimize unnecessary item swaps.
