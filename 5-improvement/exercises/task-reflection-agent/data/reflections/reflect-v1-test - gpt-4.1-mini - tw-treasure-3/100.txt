When instructed to retrieve a specific item (e.g., a passkey) from a location (e.g., a basket), then thoroughly search the current location and adjacent accessible areas for that item before moving on, rather than assuming it is not present and moving away.
When encountering locked gates or doors requiring keys, then ensure to have the corresponding key in inventory before attempting to unlock or open them, and prioritize locating the key first.
When repeatedly exploring the same set of rooms without finding the required item, then consider that the item might be in a less obvious location or that additional actions (e.g., searching containers, using different commands) may be necessary to reveal or obtain it.
