The discography of American hip hop recording artist and actor Childish Gambino comprises five studio albums, fourteen mixtapes, and four EPs. After releasing his first five mixtapes and EP independently, he signed to Glassnote Records and released Camp (2011),[1] his first album on a major record label.[2]

  4. ^ "Sweatpants" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number 24 on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles chart.[60]
  5. ^ "Sober" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number two on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles chart.[60]
  6. ^ "Me and Your Mama" did not enter the NZ Top 40 Singles Chart, but peaked at number four on the NZ Heatseekers Singles Chart.[65]
  7. ^ "Terrified" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number 25 on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles chart.[60]
  8. ^ "Terrified" did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 10 on the Bubbling Under R&B/Hip-Hop Singles chart.[69]
  9. ^ "Time" did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 24 on the Hot R&B Songs chart.[71]
  10. ^ "Time" did not enter the NZ Top 40 Singles Chart, but peaked at number 21 on the NZ Hot Singles Chart.[72]
  11. ^ "Psilocybae (Millennial Love)" did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 20 on the Hot R&B Songs chart.[71]
  12. ^ "Psilocybae (Millennial Love)" did not enter the NZ Top 40 Singles Chart, but peaked at number 22 on the NZ Hot Singles Chart under the name "12.38".[72]
  13. ^ "Lithonia" did not enter the NZ Top 40 Singles Chart, but peaked at number 14 on the NZ Hot Singles Chart.[74]
  14. ^ "In the Night" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number 17 on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles chart.[60]

  18. ^ "Jump Hi" did not enter the Flanders Ultratop 50, but peaked at number 51 on the Ultratip chart.[6]
  19. ^ "Stay High" (Childish Gambino remix) did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 21 on the Hot R&B Songs chart.[71]
  20. ^ "One Wish" did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 21 on the Hot R&B Songs chart.[71]
  21. ^ "Feels Like Summer" did not enter the NZ Top 40 Singles Chart, but peaked at number 16 on the NZ Hot Singles Chart.[86]
  22. ^ "Feels Like Summer" was included on the album 3.15.20 under the title 42.26 (the title refers to the time at which the song appears on the album).[88]
  23. ^ "Favorite Song" did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 20 on the R&B/Hip-Hop Digital Song Sales chart.[57]
  24. ^ "II. Worldstar" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number nine on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 chart, which acts as an extension to the Hot 100.[60]
  25. ^ "Break Your Heart Right Back" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number twelve on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 chart.[60]
  26. ^ "Driving Ms. Daisy" did not enter the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number six on the Bubbling Under R&B/Hip-Hop Singles chart.[69]
  27. ^ "Retro" did not enter the Billboard Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart, but peaked at number 37 on the R&B/Hip-Hop Digital Song Sales chart.[57]
  28. ^ "California" did not enter the Billboard Hot 100, but peaked at number six on the Bubbling Under Hot 100 chart.[60]
  29. ^ "California" did not enter the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs, but peaked at number one on the Bubbling Under R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart.[69]
